    Reading the map

    Filled point: Russian armed forces responsible, according to the cited body.
    Dashed ring: alleged Ukrainian responsibility, or responsibility not determined by the UN.
    Point size grows with the number of people killed. Ringed discs group nearby events: the large figure is the number of events, the small figure the deaths they contain, and the ring's colours show the mix of categories. Bigger discs hold more deaths. Click a disc to zoom in.
    Coordinates are given in decimal degrees. Each event states its precision: exact site, approximate site, settlement centre or wider area.

    How to read the figures

    Each headline death toll is the latest official count: the final Ukrainian figure where one exists, otherwise the UN figure. UN figures are minimums: they count only cases the UN has verified, and they are revised as verification continues. Where UN and Ukrainian figures differ, the event shows both.

    Every event was checked against up to three sources on 22 Oct 2026, and its coordinates against OpenStreetMap.

    "War crime" is a legal finding for courts. Where a UN body, the OSCE or the ICC has reached a conclusion, the event says so. Other events are attacks that Ukrainian prosecutors are investigating as war crimes and that the UN has condemned or recorded.
