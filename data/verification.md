## Decisions (22 Oct 2026)

- **Bucha:** headline is 422 (National Police, March 2024); OHCHR's ~50 verified killings stays in the note.
- **Figure rule:** the headline is the latest official count. Where a final Ukrainian figure exists, it leads and the UN/COI figure goes in the note. This changed Kramatorsk 2022 (61, COI 59), Sumy 2025 (35, HRMMU 31) and Olenivka (53, UN ≥50).
- **Mariupol theatre:** about 300 (City Council estimate), labelled as an estimate; the AP's ~600 is in the note.
- **Scope:** Makiivka and the Donetsk market stay on the map, marked as not attributed to Russian forces.
- **Child counts:** the label shows the lower figure as "at least" (Ternopil at least 7, with 8 in the note; Uman at least 4, with 3 in the note).
- **Kharkiv, 15 Jun 2026:** 5 killed (4 rescuers and a city emergency worker); the UN's 4 is in the note.
- **Mariupol maternity hospital:** marker stays at the city centre, labelled as exact site not confirmed.
- **Kyiv region, 5 Aug 2026:** marker at Kvitneve/Brovary, where most deaths occurred, marked as an area.
- **Kramatorsk buses, 8 Oct 2026:** 33 killed; 22 injured (regional governor), marked preliminary, with 18 (State Emergency Service) in the note.
- **Serhiivka:** both missile types (Kh-22 and Kh-31) are noted; the description does not name one.
- **Pryluky:** 22 killed in the town (State Emergency Service final count); the UN's regional figure of 19 is in the note.
